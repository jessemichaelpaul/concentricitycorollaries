## Pinpointed statements (VERBATIM)
### Thomason Thm 1.2, as quoted by Sharma as his Theorem 1.1
Sharma's lead-in sentence (Section 1, immediately before the theorem):
> In the paper [Tho79] it was shown that the homotopy type of the homotopy colimit of a diagram of categories is encoded in the Grothendieck construction of the diagram. We first recall this result:

The theorem as printed in Sharma (Section 1; math transcribed from the ar5iv MathML alttext, i.e. Sharma's LaTeX):
> **Theorem 1.1.** [Tho79, Thm. 1.2] Let $F:D\to\mathbf{Cat}$ be a functor taking values in categories. There is a natural homotopy equivalence of spaces:
> $$\lvert hocolim{\ NF}\rvert\overset{\sim}{\to}\mathcal{B}\left(\int^{{{d}}\in{D}}{F}({{d}})\right),$$
> between (geometric realization of) the homotopy colimit of $NF$ and the classifying space of the Grothendieck construction of $F$.

(location: Section 1 (introduction) of arXiv:2205.13686, ar5iv rendering; element id `S1.Thmthm1`. Rendered display: |hocolim NF| ~-> B(integral^{d in D} F(d)). NOTE: this is Sharma's quotation of Thomason; attribution tag "[Tho79, Thm. 1.2]" is Sharma's own, printed as part of the theorem header.)

### Sharma's framing of Thomason's classical result (Section 1, first paragraph)
> We thereby generalize Thomason's classical result which states that the homotopy colimit of a diagram of categories has the homotopy type of (the classifying space of) the Grothendieck construction of the diagram of categories.

(location: Section 1, opening paragraph of arXiv:2205.13686, ar5iv rendering; same sentence appears in the arXiv abstract context.)
