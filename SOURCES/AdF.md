> **Remark 2.23.** Notice that if $f$ is $\mathbb{C}_{I}$-preserving for some $I\in\mathbb{S}$, then we have $f_{v}=f_{1}I$ with $f_{1}$ slice preserving. This entails

### Definition 2.24 (arXiv numbering) — the *-logarithm
> **Definition 2.24.** We set
> $$\mathcal{R}^{*}(\Omega)\quad:=\{g\in\mathcal{R}(\Omega)\,|\,g\mbox{ is never vanishing}\},$$
> $$\mathcal{R}^{1}(\Omega)\quad:=\{g\in\mathcal{R}(\Omega)\,|\,g^{s}\equiv 1\}.$$
> Given $g\in\mathcal{R}^{*}(\Omega)$, a function $f\in\mathcal{R}(\Omega)$ such that $\exp_{*}(f)=g$ is said to be a $*$-logarithm of $g$.

### Theorem 6.17 (same number in arXiv and journal versions)
> **Theorem 6.17.** Let $\Omega$ be slice contractible and $g\in\mathcal{R}^{1}(\Omega)$ be such that for any $q_{0}\in\Omega$ that is a non-real isolated zero of $g_{v}$ we have $g_{0}(q_{0})=1$. Then, on every connected component of $\Omega\setminus g_{0}^{-1}((-\infty,-1])$, there exists a $*$-logarithm of $g$.
