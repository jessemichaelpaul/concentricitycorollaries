## 1. The action precedes the diagram

C1--C4 determine the vertically integrated distinguished
function/Möbius element. For an input `s ∈ 𝕆*`, its normalization selects
the sphere and coordinate where that element acts.

Orbit--stabilizer and `G₂` make the same action functorial on projective
frames and throughout `SphereWorld`.

Only after that global action exists is it curried over the projective base:

```text
F_A : β ⥤ Grpd.
```

`F_A` is required by the generic Grothendieck-components engine. It is not:

- the sectional projection `AsectionSlice : β ⥤ SphereWorld`;
- a constant `Grpd.of SphereWorld` wrapper;
- a fixed carrier decorated by A;
- an independently designed graph or product.

Semantically, `F_A(X)` is the groupoid of all intrinsic value states
generated by the one action positioned at `X`. `F_A.map f` is the
corresponding invertible transport of that whole action.

The exact Lean representation is Gate 1's audit subject.

## 2. The total

After `F_A` is approved:

```text
T_A := Grothendieck (F_A ⋙ Grpd.forgetToCat).
```

An object remembers its projective base point and an action-generated value
state. A morphism remembers a base motion and the induced value transport.

The total records the already unified function/Möbius action. It does not
fuse two independently constructed natures.
