## Standing discriminator

A green declaration certifies only its literal type. It certifies a gate
only when:

1. its subject is the one C1--C4 function/Möbius element;
2. normalized inputs are evaluated rather than installed;
3. object and arrow actions have the same A-specific provenance;
4. orbit--stabilizer, GPV, `G₂`, `SphereWorld`, input, and output are
   load-bearing where claimed;
5. the terminal comparison is explicit;
6. deleting a claimed dimension invalidates the receipt;
7. no downstream conclusion or reader is encoded into the carrier.

Delete-and-rebuild is a useful mechanical tripwire. Generator provenance is
the primary criterion.
